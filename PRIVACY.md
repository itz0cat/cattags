# Privacy Policy

CatTags is committed to user privacy and data minimization.

## What Information We Collect

* **Account Information**: If you register as a team owner, we store your email and a securely hashed password.
* **Team Information**: Team name, prefix, slug, description, colors, and uploaded team logo images.
* **Public Minecraft Identifiers**: Minecraft usernames and/or UUIDs associated with registered teams.
* **Ephemeral Logs**: Web requests log HTTP route, response status, and duration for security monitoring. IP addresses are temporarily logged for rate limiting and fraud prevention and are not sold or shared.

## What We Do NOT Collect

* Microsoft account credentials or Xbox tokens.
* In-game chat contents or gameplay telemetry.
* Location data or hardware identifiers.

## Data Retention & Deletion

Team administrators can remove player associations or delete their team at any time through the web dashboard, which permanently purges the team record and member associations from our database.
